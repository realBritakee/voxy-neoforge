# Voxy - NeoForge Port

**Minecraft 1.20.1 / 1.21.1 - NeoForge / Forge**

A custom NeoForge port of [Voxy](https://modrinth.com/mod/voxy) - the high-performance Level of Detail (LoD) rendering mod for Minecraft. This fork maintains NeoForge compatibility across multiple Minecraft versions.

---

## What is Voxy?

Voxy renders distant terrain at reduced detail, allowing you to see hundreds of chunks further than normal render distance without the performance cost of rendering full geometry at distance. Think Distant Horizons, but built specifically for performance and compatibility with modern rendering mods.

- See terrain and structures from 256+ chunks away
- Minimal performance impact at high LoD distances
- Seamless transition between full detail and LoD geometry
- Compatible with shaders via Iris
